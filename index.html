At a glance

Statewide trends

Every Rhode Island nursing home in CMS's monthly Care Compare files. Charts start in April 2019, the first month after CMS changed how star ratings are calculated.

Serious citations per year

Citations for actual harm or immediate jeopardy (scope/severity G-L), by inspection date. The latest year is partial.

Federal fines per year

Fines by penalty date, latest version of each fine. The latest year is partial.

What happened when homes were sold

Rhode Island's Health Department reviews each change in a nursing home's effective control. This compares each home's ratings and staffing before and after such a change, using the last CMS rating before the sale as the starting point, and compares them with homes that were not sold over the same periods.
